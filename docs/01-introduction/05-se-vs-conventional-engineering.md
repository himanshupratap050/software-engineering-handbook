## 1. Exam-Ready Definition
Software engineering follows the same *engineering principles* as conventional engineering (planning, design before construction, quality control), but its product is **logical and intangible**, so the nature of cost, failure and maintenance differs.

## 2. Core Concept
**Similarities**
- Both follow a systematic, disciplined approach.
- Both start with requirements, then plan and design before building.
- Both work under cost, time and quality constraints.
- Both use standards, reviews and testing.
- Both need teamwork and project management.
- Both require maintenance after delivery.

**Differences**

| Aspect | Conventional engineering | Software engineering |
|---|---|---|
| Product nature | Physical, tangible | Logical, intangible |
| Production | Manufacturing is a major cost | Cost is mostly design; copying is nearly free |
| Wear | Components wear out | Does not wear out, deteriorates by change |
| Reuse | Standard components widely used | Component reuse is limited |
| Change | Costly and difficult after construction | Easier to change, but changes can introduce hidden defects |
| Laws and standards | Based on mature physical laws and codes | Less mature theory and standards |
| Estimation | Better understood | Difficult and error-prone |
| Testing | Physical tests | Exhaustive testing is impossible |

## 4. Advantages, Disadvantages and Comparisons
Software's flexibility is both an advantage (easy to modify) and a risk (uncontrolled change quality problems).

## 5. Key Points for Revision
- Same principles: planning, design, quality, teamwork.
- Software: no manufacturing phase, no wear-out, hard to see.
- Cost lies in design and development.
- Estimation and standards are less mature in software.

## 8. Quick Check Questions
1. Why is the manufacturing cost of software nearly zero?
2. Which is easier to change after delivery, a bridge or software? Is that always good?
3. Name two similarities between the two engineering types.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Copying digital files costs almost nothing; the cost is in design and development.
2. Software is easier, but changes may introduce new defects.
3. Systematic approach, planning, quality control, teamwork (any two).
</details>

## 9. Common Exam Mistakes and Confusing Pairs
- Writing only differences or only similarities. Give **both**.
- Saying software "has no cost to produce". Correct: no *manufacturing* cost, but high *development* cost.
